# The Democracy of The Rupee Fund

The Rupee Fund is a community ! How is this community formed, what do we aspire it to be,
what are our principles, how we engage with each other and operate, etc - all that and
more will be covered here.

Various aspects of The Rupee Fund are covered in these documents. All of them are in
DRAFT state, and up for discussion:

- [Real Voting Mechanism](doc/REAL-VOTING.md). This refers to [Proposed - Basics of the Voting Mechanism](doc/VOTING-BASICS.md).
- [Code of Conduct](doc/CODE-OF-CONDUCT.md)
- [How The Rupee Fund compares to Other Funding Mechanisms](doc/COMPARISON.md)
- [Nomination Mechanism for Projects](doc/NOMINATION.md)
- [Karma for self-regulation](doc/KARMA.md)
- [How costs are tracked and reported](doc/COSTS.md)
- [Spaces for Community Interaction in TRF](doc/TRF-SPACES.md)
- [Why should we even do this? Roadmap](doc/ROADMAP.md)
- [Volunteering Roles potentially available](doc/VOLUNTEERING.md)
- [Details on our Infrastructure](doc/INFRA.md)

We also have a creative take on what sort of projects would be interesting. See
[doc/examples/README.md](doc/examples/README.md).

This project does not take PRs and Issues at the moment.  Please participate in the
discussions on [The Rupee Fund discussions on GitHub](https://github.com/orgs/RupeeFund/discussions)
