# Volunteering at TRF

STATE: **DRAFT**

To achieve collective goals, we will need volunteers for various
roles. Not everything on day 0, but eventually

## Outreach

TRF is a wild idea. We are improving our documentation, but all the same
it won't be easy to explain to anyone in one sitting.

We have to be able to explain the idea well to all the below folks:

- Students
- Users
- Creators
- Influencers, without paying for advertising

Word of mouth works best. Do you believe TRF can achieve it's stated goals?
Find others and make that future happen. If in doubt, ask questions.
What we have are interesting possibilities, but we must be aware that without
a critical mass, we cannot reach escape velocity!

## Thought Leaders

A lot of thought needs to go into careful design of various aspects of
The Rupee Fund.  How do we scale without adding overheads. How do we
build, sustain and multiply trust.

How do we build a healthy self regulating community. How do we ensure
everyone feels fairly treated ? And how do we ensure we don't go off
the rails ? How do we communicate this consistently both inside and
outside the community ?

The answers aren't easy. And we need thought leaders thinking about
this, leading discussions and driving change.

## Creators

We need brilliant creators - folks who can lead the community in
imagining what could be done together.  We need the people with
the brilliant and boring ideas both. We need leaders who put together
teams, by pulling in the best from the community. We need dilligent
folks with niche skills and general skills, trying to join forces
with the right teams.

Everyone has a role to play. Pick yours!

## Reviewers

Reviewer sign off is essential for high value projects. We ensure
a project is feasible when it goes for voting, not in postmortem.

How can the community be sure that a proposed project is in fact
feasible and achievable in the given budget ? That's where expert
reviewers come in.  These people are the subject matter experts
who are willing to look into a projecct in detail, and work out
feasibility in terms of budget, timeline and technology. They
help revise project plans, and flag things that need to be fixed.
