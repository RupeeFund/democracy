# Spaces in The Rupee Fund

STATE: **DRAFT**

The Rupee Fund may be considered a co-operative where members get together to run most
of the lifecycle of Free and Open Source software and Digital Public Goods.

Members can discover, co-create and co-fund great projects, build trust in the community
and get fruits of their efforts at the same time.

## Discussion Forum (on Github)

This is a space that we will be using temporarily, till our Forum gets ready.

## TRF Forum

Our forum is a reference implementation of respectful interactions we wish to encourage.
It will differ from a standard online forums (e.g. discourse) in the following ways:

1. Different spaces for "opinions" and "reasoned arguments". Opinions may be quoted
   inline in "reasoned arguments", and vice versa.
2. Karma system as the system of promoting a healthy community

## "Looking for Contributors", aka Job Board

So many projects get stuck for various reasons. Primarily in this time,
with access to complementary skills required to pull off projects. We are
in a world that rewards specialization and sythesis both. Creativity is
an essential aspect of most tasks.

We could have a list of projects in planning as a dedicated space. Basically,
a scratchpad of curious or serious ideas. Projects in planning could have
open positions. There will be enough context for members to figure out what
is being discussed, how everything fits together, what roles people are
expected to play, and so on. Positions could change with time, or evolve.
Collaborators from the community could join in, have discussions, figure
out if they can work together etc. Budgeting can be worked out bottom up,
rather than top down as a result. And when all the people are ready and
willing, the project is ready to seek community support via funding. And the
entire community can see how the project came together.
