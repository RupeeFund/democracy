## Negative Karma

Negative Karma is our crowd sourced policing mechanism, where contributors police other
contributors.  This is not the same as Reddit karma points system.

Negative Karma accrues when somebody reports that somebody else is doing something
in "bad faith" in clear violation of rules. Reporting is done through a karma reporting
system. Every negative karma report must have a well substantiated reason, and proof for
the same(URL/link). The negative karma report includes a reporter selectable delay factor -
which may not be unusually large - e.g. not more than a month or so. This exists to improve
anonimity. Karma reporters may choose to be non-anonymous as well (should we allow this?),
but they must be well aware of the complications this may cause. Reporters may opt for
instant delivery also, with care.

After the delay factor, an anonymised bad karma report is sent to the person, with the
proof. The person may refute this with contrary evidence, or accept this karma. A separate
queue manages refutations.  Poorly created refutations may generate more negative karma
from the curators of such queues. A user may be protected against negative karma bombardment.
There will be a limit to the number of negative karma reports sent out in a month for a person.
A dashboard will list all the negative karma. All negative karma is personal.

How does somebody get rid of the negative karma ? Atonement - correction, etc are possible.
The user interface will have a "resolve karma" option, with corresponding explanations, proofs,
etc. Resolution will be sent to the original karma reporter for resolution.  Only when
the original karma reporter accepts, does the negative karma dissipate.

Negative karma reports may also be withdrawn, but not without justification.  Withdrawals
are subject to reviews, and may generate negative and positive karma too!

### Empathy

Contributors should generally not rush to give negative karma. They must carefully evaluate
whether someone is doing something wilfully. Lack of understanding, miscommunication may be
treated with empathy in the initial stages. Repeat offenders may not need to be given undue
consideration.

### The language of Karma

For Karma reports and resolutions to be simple, the humans in the loop must use simple laguage,
short sentences, and avoid complex idioms. Usage of indian languages is encouraged, where-ever
appropriate. Grossly incorrect usage of english may invite negative karma as well, so
corresponders are expected to consult the dictionary judiciously prior to writing in
haste.

In general, we suggest usage of TRF spaces without switching into an activist mode.  We welcome
reasoned arguments made with data.  Pure "opinion" does not add to the discourse in meaningful
ways. As a pure outlet, we may consider a separate "opionion" channel, and a separate
"reasoned arguments" channel. Observations in the "opinion" channel may be refuted in "reasoned
arguments", and can be withdrawn.  Withdrawn opinions will still be listed somewhere. Opinions
that don't age well may come back to haunt you years later, so don't jump to them!

# Positive Karma

This may be visualized as free goodwill in the community. However, public memory is
short - hence positive karma reports are useful too.

Positive Karma reports may not be withdrawn. They happen in a specific environment, with a
specific context. So giving positive karma needs careful consideration too !

# Awards

Awards should be considered in due course of time. Positive karma reports will be
used as basis, combined with nominations, voting etc.

# No money to exchange hands for Karma

Karma is not about give and take.

Positive karma is about recognizing a good thing, beyond a simple "thanks". Negative karma
is about regulating negative communication, acts of bad faith including wilful suppression
of facts, etc.  Anybody who uses Negative karma to get "even" might invite negative karma
by that very act, if proved :)

No monetary or other considerations must form the basis of giving karma. Members may
reject positive karma too!

# How much karma can someone give?

120 karma points, each for positive and negative karmas. This is the LCM
of 24 and 60.

Karma is flexibly given in the range of 1 to 120. Limit resets after 24
hours.
